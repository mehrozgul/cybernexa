PRACTICAL LAB

Ransomware Lab

Identify ransomware characteristics, understand its impact and practice recognizing appropriate prevention and incident-response measures.

Difficulty Beginner
Type Malware Analysis
Objective Identify Ransomware Risks

Lab Objective

In this lab, you will analyze a simple ransomware scenario and identify its characteristics, possible impact and appropriate security measures.

  • Identify the main characteristics of ransomware.
  • Understand how ransomware can affect files and systems.
  • Recognize common ransomware prevention measures.
  • Understand basic incident-response actions.

Scenario

SecureTech Company

SecureTech employees suddenly report that several important documents are inaccessible. A message appears on affected systems demanding payment and claiming that the files can be restored after the demand is fulfilled. The security team must identify the incident, isolate affected systems and determine safe recovery steps.

Knowledge Check

1. What is the main purpose of ransomware?
2. What is commonly used by ransomware to make files inaccessible?
3. Which action can help reduce ransomware risk?
4. What should a security team do when ransomware is suspected?
5. Which security measure can help protect accounts from ransomware-related attacks?

Lab Result

Complete the Ransomware Lab

After completing the knowledge check, review the main ransomware concepts and remember that backups, patching, MFA, least privilege, security awareness and incident response are important parts of ransomware defense.
