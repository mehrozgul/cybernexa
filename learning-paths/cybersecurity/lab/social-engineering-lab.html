PRACTICAL LAB

Social Engineering Lab

Analyze a simulated social-engineering scenario, identify manipulation techniques and select appropriate defensive actions.

Difficulty Beginner
Type Security Awareness
Objective Detect Manipulation

Lab Objective

In this lab, you will analyze a simulated interaction and identify the social-engineering techniques being used.

  • Identify psychological manipulation indicators.
  • Recognize suspicious requests for information.
  • Understand the importance of verification.
  • Select safe defensive responses.

Scenario

SecureTech Employee

An employee receives a phone call from someone claiming to be an IT support representative. The caller says there is an urgent security problem and asks the employee to provide account information. The employee must decide how to respond safely.

Caller:

Hello, I am calling from IT support. We detected a security issue with your account and need to verify some information immediately.

Employee:

Can you provide a ticket number or official contact information so I can verify this request?

Caller:

There is no time for that. Your account may be disabled if you do not cooperate immediately.

⚠️ Training scenario: Notice the use of authority, urgency and pressure. Do not provide passwords or sensitive information based only on an unexpected request.

Knowledge Check

1. Which social-engineering technique is illustrated by pretending to be IT support?
2. Which psychological trigger is used when the caller says the account may be disabled immediately?
3. What should the employee do before trusting the request?
4. Which information should never be provided simply because an unexpected caller asks for it?
5. What is an important defense against social engineering?

Lab Result

Complete the Social Engineering Lab

After completing the knowledge check, remember that unexpected requests should be verified through trusted channels. Never provide passwords, MFA codes or other sensitive information simply because someone claims to be an authorized person.
