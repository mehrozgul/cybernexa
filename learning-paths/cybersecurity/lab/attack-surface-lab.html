PRACTICAL LAB

Attack Surface Lab

Identify exposed assets, recognize possible attack entry points and practice reducing an organization's attack surface.

Difficulty Beginner
Type Security Analysis
Objective Identify Attack Surface

Lab Objective

In this lab, you will analyze a simple organization scenario and identify which assets are part of its attack surface.

  • Identify exposed assets.
  • Recognize possible entry points.
  • Understand unnecessary exposure.
  • Select appropriate attack-surface reduction measures.

Scenario

SecureTech Company

SecureTech has a public website, an internet-facing web server, several network services, employee accounts and cloud storage. Your task is to identify the assets that could form part of the company's attack surface and determine how exposure can be reduced.

Knowledge Check

1. What does an attack surface represent?
2. Which of the following can be part of a digital attack surface?
3. Which action can reduce an organization's attack surface?
4. Why should unnecessary network ports be closed?
5. Which is an example of an attack-surface asset?

Lab Result

Complete the Attack Surface Lab

After completing the knowledge check, review which assets create exposure and remember that reducing unnecessary entry points is an important part of cybersecurity.
