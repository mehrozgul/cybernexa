PRACTICAL LAB

MFA Lab

Identify authentication factors, understand how MFA works and recognize secure multi-factor authentication practices.

Difficulty Beginner
Type Authentication Security
Objective Understand MFA

Lab Objective

In this lab, you will identify different authentication factors and understand how MFA provides additional protection for user accounts.

  • Identify common authentication factors.
  • Understand the purpose of MFA.
  • Distinguish MFA from single-factor authentication.
  • Recognize secure MFA practices.

Scenario

SecurePortal Account

A user logs into SecurePortal using a password. The system then asks the user to confirm the login using an authenticator application. Your task is to identify the authentication factors and understand why the second verification step improves account security.

Knowledge Check

1. What does MFA require?
2. Which is an example of "something you know"?
3. Which is an example of "something you have"?
4. Which is an example of "something you are"?
5. Why does MFA improve account security?
6. Which statement about MFA and 2FA is correct?

Lab Result

Complete the MFA Lab

After completing the knowledge check, review the authentication factors and remember that MFA adds an additional layer of protection beyond passwords.
