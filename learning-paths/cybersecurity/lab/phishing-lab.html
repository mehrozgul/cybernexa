PRACTICAL LAB

Phishing Lab

Learn how to identify common phishing indicators, analyze suspicious messages and apply safe defensive practices.

Difficulty Beginner
Type Threat Analysis
Objective Identify Phishing

🎯 Lab Objective

In this lab, you will analyze a simulated suspicious message and identify common phishing indicators.

  • Understand common phishing warning signs.
  • Identify suspicious links and sender information.
  • Recognize urgency and social-engineering techniques.
  • Practice safe responses to suspicious messages.

🧩 Scenario

SecureTech Employee

You receive a message claiming to be from your organization's IT department. The message says that your account will be disabled soon unless you verify your account immediately. You must analyze the message before taking any action.

Sender: IT Support <support@example-security-check.com>
Subject: Urgent: Verify Your Account Immediately
Message: Your account requires immediate verification. Failure to verify may result in account suspension. Please use the provided verification link and confirm your account information.
Link: http://example-security-check.com/verify
⚠️ This is a simulated training message. Do not enter real credentials or personal information into suspicious websites.

🧪 Knowledge Check

1. Which sign is commonly associated with phishing?
2. What should you check when analyzing a suspicious email?
3. What should you do if an unexpected message asks for your password?
4. Why can urgent language be a phishing warning sign?
5. What is the safest response to a suspicious link?

Lab Result

✅ Complete the Phishing Lab

After completing the knowledge check, remember to verify unexpected requests, inspect sender information, avoid suspicious links and report suspected phishing through the appropriate security channel.
