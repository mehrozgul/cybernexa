PRACTICAL LAB

Authorization Lab

Practice authorization concepts by identifying roles, permissions and appropriate access decisions in a safe simulated environment.

Difficulty Beginner
Type Access Control
Objective Understand Authorization

Lab Objective

In this lab, you will practice deciding whether a user should be allowed to perform a specific action based on their assigned role and permissions.

  • Understand the difference between authentication and authorization.
  • Identify roles and permissions.
  • Apply the principle of least privilege.
  • Understand basic RBAC decisions.

Scenario

CyberNexa Learning Portal

The CyberNexa portal has three roles: Administrator, Instructor and Student. Each role has different permissions. Your task is to determine whether the requested actions should be allowed according to the user's role.

Knowledge Check

1. What is the main purpose of authorization?
2. Which authorization model assigns permissions based on roles?
3. A Student can view their own grades but cannot change them. What concept does this demonstrate?
4. Which user should normally have permission to manage system settings?
5. What is the difference between authentication and authorization?
6. An Instructor needs to manage course material but does not need to manage user accounts. What should the system provide?

Lab Result

Complete the Authorization Lab

After completing the knowledge check, remember that authorization controls what authenticated users are allowed to access. Use roles, permissions and least privilege to reduce unnecessary access.
