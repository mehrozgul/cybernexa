PRACTICAL LAB

Spyware Lab

Identify spyware behavior, understand how spyware can affect users and systems, and learn basic defensive measures against spyware threats.

Difficulty Beginner
Type Malware Analysis
Objective Identify Spyware

🎯 Lab Objective

In this lab, you will analyze basic spyware concepts and identify behaviors that may indicate unauthorized monitoring or information collection.

  • Understand what spyware is.
  • Identify common spyware behaviors.
  • Understand the risks associated with spyware.
  • Recognize basic defensive measures.

🧩 Scenario

SecureTech Employee Laptop

An employee notices that a computer is behaving unusually. Unknown software is running in the background and unexpected network communication is occurring. Security monitoring also shows that an application may be collecting user information without clear permission. Your task is to determine which behavior could indicate spyware and identify suitable defensive actions.

🧪 Knowledge Check

1. What is spyware?
2. Which behavior can be associated with spyware?
3. Which information may spyware attempt to collect?
4. Which action can help protect a system from spyware?
5. Why is spyware dangerous?

Lab Result

✅ Complete the Spyware Lab

After completing the knowledge check, review how spyware can affect privacy and information security. Remember that updated systems, trusted software sources, security controls and user awareness can help reduce spyware-related risks.
