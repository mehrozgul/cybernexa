Virus Analysis Lab

Test your understanding of computer viruses, their behavior, infection methods, characteristics and basic defensive practices.

Level Beginner
Category Malware Analysis
Questions 8 Questions
Environment Safe Lab

Lab Scenario

You are working as a junior cybersecurity analyst. Your task is to identify the characteristics and behavior of computer viruses and recognize the appropriate security practices used to reduce virus-related risks.

⚠ Safety Notice

Do not download or execute real computer viruses. This lab is completely theoretical and uses safe scenarios for cybersecurity learning.

1. What is a computer virus?

2. What is a common characteristic of a computer virus?

3. How can a virus commonly spread from one computer to another?

4. What usually needs to happen before a traditional file-infecting virus can become active?

5. Which type of virus can infect executable program files?

6. Which practice can help reduce the risk of virus infection?

7. Why should suspicious files be scanned before opening them?

8. You receive an unexpected executable attachment from an unknown sender. What should you do?

How to Complete This Lab

  1. Read each virus-analysis question carefully.
  2. Select one answer for every question.
  3. Click "Check My Answers".
  4. Review your score and identify concepts that need revision.
  5. Return to the Virus theory page if you need to review the topic.

Lab Completed?

After completing this lab, you should understand what a computer virus is, how viruses can spread, how infected files can become active and which basic security practices can reduce virus-related risks.
