PRACTICAL LAB

Encryption Lab

Understand how encryption protects information, distinguish plaintext from ciphertext and identify the role of cryptographic keys.

Difficulty Beginner
Type Cryptography
Objective Understand Encryption

Lab Objective

In this lab, you will test your understanding of basic encryption concepts and learn how encrypted information protects confidentiality.

  • Identify plaintext and ciphertext.
  • Understand the purpose of an encryption key.
  • Distinguish symmetric and asymmetric encryption.
  • Understand the difference between encryption and hashing.

Scenario

SecureData Organization

SecureData stores confidential customer information and sends sensitive data between its systems. The security team wants to protect this information so that unauthorized users cannot understand the data if it is intercepted or accessed without authorization. Your task is to identify how encryption can help protect the information.

Knowledge Check

1. What is the main purpose of encryption?
2. What is readable data before encryption called?
3. What is the protected output of encryption called?
4. Which type of encryption uses the same secret key for encryption and decryption?
5. Which statement correctly describes hashing?
6. Which type of encryption uses a public key and a private key?

Lab Result

Complete the Encryption Lab

After completing the knowledge check, review the difference between plaintext and ciphertext, understand the role of cryptographic keys and remember that encryption is primarily used to protect confidentiality.
