Task 1 — Understand Risk

In cybersecurity, risk can be estimated using likelihood and impact. In this lab, you will select values for both and calculate the resulting risk score.

Risk Assessment

Select a likelihood value and an impact value. The system will calculate the risk score using: Risk = Likelihood × Impact.

Practical Scenario

An organization has an internet-facing server with a known vulnerability. Security staff estimate the likelihood as 4 and the potential impact as 5.

Therefore:

Likelihood 4
Impact 5
Risk Score 20

This demonstrates how a higher likelihood combined with a higher impact produces a higher risk score.
