What is Cybersecurity?

Protection of digital systems, networks, and data from threats. Learn the core concepts in simple notes format.

01 Definition

📖 Definition

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, programs, and data from digital attacks, unauthorized access, damage, or theft.

  • Also called information security or IT security
  • Covers both hardware and software protection
  • Includes people, processes, and technology
  • Goal: keep digital systems safe and working correctly

02 Why Cybersecurity Matters

  • 🔐 Protects personal data (passwords, financial info, medical records)
  • 🏢 Keeps businesses running — cyberattacks cost millions per incident
  • 🌐 Secures critical infrastructure (power grids, hospitals, banks)
  • 🛡️ Prevents identity theft and financial fraud
  • 📱 Secures devices we use every day
  • ⚖️ Required by laws and regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, etc.)

03 The CIA Triad — Core Principles

Everything in cybersecurity is built around 3 principles:

🔒
Confidentiality
Only authorized people can see the data. Example: encrypted files, password protection.
✅
Integrity
Data is accurate and not tampered with. Example: checksums, digital signatures.
🌐
Availability
Systems are available when needed. Example: backups, DDoS protection, uptime.

04 What Does Cybersecurity Protect?

  • 💻 Computers & Devices — laptops, desktops, mobile phones, servers
  • 🌐 Networks — routers, switches, Wi-Fi, internet connections
  • 📦 Applications — web apps, mobile apps, software programs
  • 🗄️ Data & Databases — personal info, business data, government records
  • ☁️ Cloud Systems — online storage, cloud services, SaaS platforms
  • 🏭 Critical Infrastructure — power grids, water systems, healthcare

05 How Cybersecurity Works

It follows a cycle — not a one-time setup:

  • 🔍 Identify — find assets, risks, and threats
  • 🛡️ Protect — apply firewalls, encryption, access control
  • 👁️ Detect — monitor systems for suspicious activity
  • ⚡ Respond — handle incidents quickly to reduce damage
  • 🔄 Recover — restore systems and improve defenses
💡 Remember

These 5 steps are from the NIST Cybersecurity Framework — a widely used standard by companies worldwide.

06 Real-World Example

🌍 Example

A hospital stores patient records. If hackers steal these records, it violates confidentiality. If they change drug dosage records, it violates integrity. If they shut down the system, it violates availability. Cybersecurity prevents all three.

07 Key Concepts to Remember

  • Cybersecurity = protection of digital assets from threats
  • 3 core principles: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability (CIA)
  • Threats include: hackers, malware, phishing, ransomware
  • Controls include: firewalls, encryption, MFA, antivirus
  • No system is 100% secure — goal is to reduce risk
  • Both humans and technology are part of cybersecurity
  • Regular updates and monitoring are essential

Summary

Cybersecurity protects digital systems, networks, and data from attacks and unauthorized access.

Built on the CIA Triad: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability.

Uses a cycle: Identify → Protect → Detect → Respond → Recover.

Covers devices, networks, apps, data, cloud, and critical infrastructure.
