What is Cybersecurity?

Learn the fundamental concept of cybersecurity, why it is important, what it protects, and how security controls help protect digital systems, networks, applications and information.

01. What is Cybersecurity?

Definition

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, devices, systems and data from unauthorized access, misuse, damage, disruption or theft.

In simple words, cybersecurity means keeping digital systems and information safe from security threats. It combines technologies, processes and security practices to reduce security risks.

02. Why Cybersecurity Matters

Modern organizations depend heavily on computers, networks, cloud services and digital information. A security incident can affect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of these resources.

🔒

Protect Data

Helps protect sensitive information from unauthorized access, disclosure and theft.

🛡

Protect Systems

Helps defend computers, servers, networks and applications against security threats.

🌐

Secure Communication

Security controls help protect information while it moves between connected systems.

03. What Does Cybersecurity Protect?

Computers & Devices

Protects laptops, desktops, mobile devices, servers and other computing devices.

Networks

Protects network infrastructure and the communication between connected systems.

Applications

Helps protect software and web applications from unauthorized or malicious activity.

Data & Information

Protects personal, business and sensitive information from unauthorized use or theft.

04. How Cybersecurity Works

Cybersecurity does not depend on a single tool. Organizations normally use multiple security controls together to protect their systems.

Identify important systems and information.
Identify possible threats and vulnerabilities.
Apply security controls to reduce risk.
Monitor systems for suspicious activity.
Detect and respond to security incidents.
Recover systems and improve security.

05. Real-World Example

Example

Imagine a company that stores customer information on a server. The company can use strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, firewalls, encryption, access controls and monitoring to protect that information. These controls work together as part of the organization's cybersecurity approach.

06. Key Concepts

Cybersecurity protects digital resources.
Security threats can target people, systems and data.
Security controls help reduce cybersecurity risk.
Authentication controls who can access a system.
Encryption helps protect information from unauthorized reading.
Monitoring helps identify suspicious activity.

07. Summary

What you should remember

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting digital systems, networks, applications, devices and information from security threats. It uses multiple technologies, processes and security controls to reduce risk and protect important digital resources.
