01

Investigation Brief

A digital forensic investigation focuses on identifying, collecting, examining and documenting digital evidence in a structured manner.

In this training lab, you will review basic forensic concepts and answer questions about evidence, integrity, investigation methodology and responsible handling.

💾

Digital Evidence

Data that may help establish facts about an incident or activity.

🔐

Integrity

Evidence should remain unchanged and its integrity should be protected.

📋

Documentation

Investigators record actions, observations and relevant findings.

02

Basic Forensic Workflow

01
Identification Determine what systems, devices or data may contain relevant evidence.
02
Collection Acquire relevant evidence using an appropriate and controlled procedure.
03
Preservation Protect evidence from accidental or unauthorized modification.
04
Examination Extract and organize relevant information from the collected evidence.
05
Analysis Interpret the available evidence and determine its significance.
06
Reporting Present findings, methodology and supporting evidence clearly.

Investigation Notice

⚠ CONTROLLED FORENSIC ENVIRONMENT

Practical forensic activities should be performed only on systems, devices and datasets that you own or are explicitly authorized to investigate. For learning, use dedicated forensic images, virtual machines, CTF environments and training datasets.
