03

Authorized Scenario

TRAINING ENVIRONMENT

You are working as a security trainee inside a dedicated cybersecurity lab. Your instructor gives you an approved virtual machine and its documentation. Your task is to identify what information is available about the machine, organize the findings and explain which information would be useful for the next phase of the security assessment.

The correct professional approach is to remain inside the provided scope, document observations and avoid interacting with systems that are not part of the authorized laboratory.
