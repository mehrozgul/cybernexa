Incident Response Lifecycle Lab

Practice the complete incident response lifecycle by analyzing a simulated compromised account and working through each response phase step by step.

Lab Objective

Complete each stage of the Incident Response Lifecycle.

Your Mission

You are working as a junior security analyst. Your monitoring system has detected suspicious activity involving an employee account.

Your task is to follow the complete incident response lifecycle and determine what actions should be performed at every stage.

Account: employee01
Event: Multiple failed login attempts
Successful Login: Yes
Location: Unusual geographic location
Risk: Possible account compromise

Phase 01 — Preparation

Preparation happens before an incident occurs.

Security Team Preparation

Before investigating an incident, the organization should have appropriate policies, tools, communication channels and trained personnel ready.

TASK

Select the items that are useful for incident response preparation.

Phase 02 — Detection

Identify the suspicious activity that may represent an incident.

Alert Detected

The monitoring system reports several failed authentication attempts followed by a successful login from an unusual geographic location.

TASK

Mark the event as suspicious and continue to analysis.

Phase 03 — Analysis

Investigate the alert and determine its potential impact.

Investigation

Review authentication logs, timestamps, source locations and account activity to determine whether the successful login was legitimate.

Phase 04 — Containment

Limit the possible impact of the compromised account.

Containment Actions

If the account is confirmed or strongly suspected to be compromised, appropriate containment actions should be taken to prevent further unauthorized activity.

SELECT APPROPRIATE ACTIONS

Phase 05 — Eradication

Remove the underlying cause of the incident.

Remove the Threat

After containment, the security team determines whether the account was compromised and removes unauthorized access mechanisms or other identified causes.

POSSIBLE ACTIONS

Phase 06 — Recovery

Return the affected account and services to normal operation.

Restore Normal Operation

After the threat has been addressed, the account can be restored according to organizational procedures. Security monitoring should continue to identify any repeated suspicious activity.

RECOVERY CHECKLIST

Phase 07 — Post-Incident Review

Learn from the incident and improve future response.

Lessons Learned

The security team documents what happened, what actions were performed and what improvements should be made.

FINAL CHECKLIST
