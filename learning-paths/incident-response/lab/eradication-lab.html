Lab Scenario

Read the scenario before beginning the eradication process.

Compromised Workstation

A security team has investigated a workstation after detecting suspicious activity. The investigation found a malicious file, an unauthorized persistence mechanism and a potentially compromised user credential. Your task is to perform the eradication process in the correct order and verify that the identified threats have been removed.

Eradication Procedure

Follow this logical sequence when performing eradication.

STEP 01

Review Evidence

Use investigation findings to understand which components are associated with the incident.

STEP 02

Remove Threat

Remove identified malicious files, software and unauthorized components.

STEP 03

Remove Persistence

Eliminate identified mechanisms that could allow the threat to return.

STEP 04

Remediate

Reset compromised credentials and correct the vulnerability or configuration issue.

STEP 05

Verify

Perform checks to confirm that the identified malicious activity has been removed.

STEP 06

Document

Record the eradication actions, findings and remaining recommendations.

⚠ Security Note

This CyberNexa lab is a controlled educational simulation. The interface does not execute malware-removal commands or modify the learner's real system. In a real incident, eradication actions should be performed according to the organization's incident-response procedures and appropriate authorization.

Lab Completion

Complete all six checklist tasks and run the simulation. After completing the lab, you should understand the main purpose of eradication: removing the identified threat, eliminating persistence, addressing compromised credentials and fixing the conditions that allowed the incident.

A successful eradication process should be followed by verification before the affected environment proceeds toward recovery.
