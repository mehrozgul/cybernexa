What is Incident Response?

Incident Response is the organized process used by security teams to detect, analyze, contain, eradicate and recover from cybersecurity incidents.

Definition

First, understand the core meaning of Incident Response.

Core Definition

Incident Response is a structured cybersecurity process for identifying, investigating, containing, eliminating and recovering from security incidents while reducing their impact on an organization.

How Incident Response Works

Security teams follow an organized process rather than reacting randomly to an incident.

A cybersecurity incident can involve malware, unauthorized access, suspicious login activity, data exposure, compromised accounts or other security events.

When an incident is detected, the response team first determines what happened and how serious the situation is.

The team then takes appropriate actions to limit the damage, remove the underlying threat, restore affected systems and document the incident.

The goal is not simply to stop an attack. A proper response also helps the organization understand what happened and improve its future security.

Why Incident Response Matters

A structured response can help organizations control the impact of security incidents.

REASON 01

Reduce Damage

Rapid and organized response can help limit the spread and impact of an incident.

REASON 02

Understand the Incident

Investigation helps determine what happened, when it happened and which systems were affected.

REASON 03

Restore Operations

Recovery procedures help return affected systems and services to normal operation.

Simple Example

Consider a suspicious account login as an example.

Possible Incident

A security monitoring system detects multiple failed login attempts followed by a successful login from an unusual location.

Response

The security team investigates the event, determines its severity, applies appropriate containment, checks for additional suspicious activity and documents the findings.

Key Concepts

Important terms to remember for this topic.

Incident: A security event that may affect the confidentiality, integrity or availability of systems or data.
Detection: Identifying suspicious or potentially harmful activity.
Triage: Evaluating and prioritizing an incident.
Containment: Actions taken to limit the incident's impact.
Eradication: Removing the underlying cause or threat.
Recovery: Returning affected systems to normal operation.

Summary

Incident Response is a structured approach to handling cybersecurity incidents. It involves detecting the incident, understanding its scope, prioritizing the response, containing the problem, removing the threat, recovering affected systems and documenting the investigation. Effective Incident Response helps organizations reduce impact and improve their security processes.
