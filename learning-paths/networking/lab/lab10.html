NETWORKING PRACTICAL LAB

DHCP Practical Lab

In this lab, you will inspect your device's DHCP configuration, release the current DHCP lease, request a new lease, and verify the network configuration provided by the DHCP server.

Difficulty
Beginner
Platform
Windows
Tool
Command Prompt
Topic
DHCP / Networking

Lab Objectives

  • Identify the DHCP configuration of your computer.
  • Identify the IP address, subnet mask, default gateway and DNS server.
  • Release the current DHCP lease.
  • Request a new DHCP lease from the DHCP server.
  • Verify the new network configuration.
  • Understand how the practical activity relates to the DHCP DORA process.
#

Requirements

Before starting the lab, make sure your computer is connected to a network through Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

Important You should perform the release and renew commands only on your own computer. Your network connection may temporarily disconnect while the DHCP lease is being renewed.
01
Open Command Prompt

Open Windows Command Prompt. You can press:

Windows + R

Then type:

cmd

Press Enter to open Command Prompt.

02
Check Current DHCP Configuration

Run the following command:

ipconfig /all
Example Output
Ethernet adapter Wi-Fi: DHCP Enabled. . . . . . . . . . . : Yes IPv4 Address. . . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.20 Subnet Mask . . . . . . . . . . . : 255.255.255.0 Default Gateway . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.1 DHCP Server . . . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.1 DNS Servers . . . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.1

Find these values in your own output:

Note The example IP address above is only an example. Your actual IP address will depend on your network.
03
Release the DHCP Lease

The following command tells Windows to release the current DHCP-assigned IP configuration.

ipconfig /release
What You May Observe
Windows IP Configuration Successfully released the IP address.
What happened? The computer releases its current DHCP lease. Network connectivity may temporarily stop because the adapter no longer has its previous DHCP configuration.
04
Request a New DHCP Lease

Now request a new IP configuration from the DHCP server.

ipconfig /renew
Example
Windows IP Configuration Successfully renewed the IP address.
What happened? Windows requested network configuration from the DHCP service. The DHCP server can provide an IP address and other configuration information.
05
Verify the New Configuration

Run the following command again:

ipconfig /all

Check the following values again:

IPv4 Address
Subnet Mask
Default Gateway
DNS Server
Important The IP address after renewal may be the same as the previous address or may be different. This depends on the DHCP server's lease and address allocation process.
06
Understand the DHCP DORA Process

The DHCP address assignment process is commonly described using four steps called DORA.

D

Discover

Client searches for an available DHCP server.

O

Offer

DHCP server offers network configuration.

R

Request

Client requests the offered configuration.

A

Acknowledge

Server confirms the IP configuration.

Practical Connection The ipconfig /renew command causes the Windows DHCP client to obtain network configuration through DHCP. The complete protocol exchange may involve the DORA process.
07
Identify DHCP Ports

DHCP uses UDP for its standard client-server communication.

UDP

Port 67

DHCP server port.

UDP

Port 68

DHCP client port.

08
Optional DHCP Verification

Run the following command to view detailed network configuration again:

ipconfig /all

Confirm that DHCP is enabled on the active adapter.

Look For
DHCP Enabled. . . . . . . . . . . : Yes
